## 2025 / 09 / 12

> v1.0.0-beta

Requires LeaferJS [v1.9.7](https://www.leaferjs.com/ui/update/#_2025-09-11)

#### 🌱 Initial Release

- 🌸 Keeps animations rendering even when the browser tab is in the background
- 🌸 Prevents tasks such as image and video export from being interrupted
- Configurable maximum rendering frame rate to balance performance and resource consumption
- 🌸 Supports enabling / disabling background rendering at any time
- Seamlessly switches between background and foreground rendering for a consistent experience
